The Blueprint must describe the following audio-specific properties:

- acoustic event structure (heart sounds, murmurs, lung sounds, etc.);
- temporal organization and timing patterns;
- spectral characteristics and dominant frequency bands;
- frequency ranges and amplitude relationships between components;
- envelope or amplitude-modulation behavior;
- recording or acquisition variability;
- modality-specific discriminative features that distinguish this condition from confusable conditions in the audio domain.
