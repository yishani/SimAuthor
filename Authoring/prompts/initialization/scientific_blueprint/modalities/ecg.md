The Blueprint must describe the following ECG-specific properties:

- rhythm and cycle organization;
- P-wave, QRS, and T-wave morphology where applicable;
- PR, QRS, QT/QTc, and RR intervals;
- amplitude and polarity;
- lead relationships (two leads: lead I and lead II);
- beat-to-beat variability;
- modality-specific discriminative features that distinguish this condition from confusable conditions in the ECG domain.
