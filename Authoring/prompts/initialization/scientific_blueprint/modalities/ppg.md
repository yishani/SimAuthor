The Blueprint must describe the following PPG-specific properties:

- pulse morphology and waveform shape;
- pulse interval and heart-rate behavior;
- systolic rise and diastolic decay characteristics;
- pulse width;
- dicrotic-notch characteristics where applicable;
- amplitude and baseline behavior;
- acquisition variability;
- modality-specific discriminative features that distinguish this condition from confusable conditions in the PPG domain.
